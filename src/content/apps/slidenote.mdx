SlideNote started as a personal itch: every other note app wanted me to open a window, pick a folder, and sign in before I could write a single line. I just wanted somewhere to *put a thought* — instantly, then gone.

## Built for the in-between moments

The fastest note is the one you never have to go looking for. SlideNote lives at the edge of your screen and answers to a single keystroke, so capturing a phone number, a code snippet, or a half-formed idea never means breaking your flow. When you’re done, it slides away.

## Yours, and only yours

No account to create, no server to trust, no sync you didn’t ask for. Your notes are plain files on your own Mac — fast to search, easy to back up, and impossible for anyone else to read.
